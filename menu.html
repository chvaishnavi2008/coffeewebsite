Our Process

The Seed-to-Cup Roadmap

Trace the meticulous stages of creation, where raw elements transform into your perfect morning ritual.

1

01. Ethical Harvesting

Our coffee cherries are hand-picked at peak deep-red ripeness by organic growers on sustainable micro-lots.

Sourcing
2

02. Honey & Wet Processing

The pulp is removed and beans are naturally sun-dried on raised African beds for clean, fruit-forward acidity.

Processing
3

03. Small-Batch Roasting

Beans are convection drum-roasted at specific thermal curves to develop rich caramel sugars and single-origin notes.

Craft
4

04. High-Precision Grinding

Flat-burr mills grind beans down to precise microns immediately before extraction to optimize contact surface.

Milling
5

05. Master Extraction

Tamped at a consistent 30lbs pressure and pulled for exactly 28 seconds of golden extraction.

Cup
Step 01

From carefully selected beans...

Our journey begins in the high-altitude cloud forests, where hand-picked red cherries are selected at their peak ripeness.

Step 02

To precise, artisanal roasting...

Using slow-temperature curves, we bring the beans to their peak expression, unlocking deep, rich notes of cacao and warm spices.

Step 03

To the perfect pour.

Crafted by our master baristas who measure extraction down to the milligram, serving you pure emotional liquid gold.

The Craft

Freshly brewed perfection.

Every cup is prepared in real-time with precision and passion. We do not rush. We do not compromise. We celebrate the luxury of time.

Freshly Ground Beans

Beans are ground just seconds before brewing to retain volatile aromatic compounds.

Barista-Crafted Precision

Tamped at a consistent 30lbs pressure and pulled for exactly 28 seconds of golden extraction.

Served at Perfect Temperature

Milk micro-foam structured precisely at 65°C for maximum sweet creaminess without burning.
